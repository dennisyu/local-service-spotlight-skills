# Decision registry — evidence of the owner's scoped decisions

Record an actual owner decision in the same session so the next worker does not
ask for that same decision again. Preserve its source, exact scope, limits and
current status. An agent-authored summary is not the original human record.
Verify the original evidence before acting on a consequential or disputed grant.
If it is unavailable, mark the authority UNVERIFIED and stop dependent action.

## Entry template

### D-YYYY-MM-DD-01 — short title

- **Decision and source:** exact permitted quote or reference to its private original.
- **Source identity and revision:** who decided, original record location/revision.
- **When / where:** date, time with timezone and channel.
- **Scope:** task, property, recipient/data class and actions covered.
- **Limits and holds:** budget, frequency, access, expiry and explicit exclusions.
- **Reversibility / recovery:** what can be undone and how.
- **Status:** current, superseded, revoked or UNVERIFIED, with evidence.
- **Verification:** who read the original and when; unresolved contradiction if any.

This is an empty format, not a decision or grant. A worker cites an existing
verified decision ID only when the proposed action falls inside its actual scope.
Routine reversible work still needs recorded authority; consequential or new
scope goes to the owner or designated decision holder. Silence, a broad category
label or this template supplies no new privilege and clears no platform gate.
Keep private source evidence private and never store secret values in the registry.
