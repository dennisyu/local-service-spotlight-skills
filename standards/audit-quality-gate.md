## Audit deliverables preserve evidence and pass independent quality review

- Apply the [Universal Audit Quality Gate](https://github.com/dennisyu/local-service-spotlight-skills/blob/main/docs/audit-quality/README.md) to content, document, SEO, website and operational audit deliverables. Pin the actual standard/profile revision and adoption state. It assesses report quality; keep specialist exam weights, caps, coverage and pass lines separate.
- Preserve valid detail, proof, examples, citations and meaningful visuals. Map baseline items to final locations; justify corrections, outdated/incorrect removals and authorized privacy omissions. More than 10% extracted-text loss or any missing substantive item triggers semantic review, not an automatic verdict.
- Trace material claims to exact dated sources; distinguish FACT, DECIDED, PROPOSED and UNKNOWN from OBSERVED, REPORTED and INFERRED evidence. Missing evidence is null, never zero. Inspect hidden artifact content and public links for privacy.
- Fix authorized defects at the source, propagate to every dependent output, and retest the actual final artifacts. Require fresh-context content/privacy and design review of the exact final revision. Preserve all applicable holds and consequential human decision thresholds; do routine authorized work without unnecessary review requests.
- The proposed default quality profile uses weights 25/25/20/15/10/5 and threshold 85, plus hard gates. Calibrate before operational scoring; uncalibrated scores stay provisional. No critical/major defect or missing required evidence passes through a high score.
- Release through one authorized owner using current destination-specific access, preimage and rollback. Verify private recipient delivery or anonymous public body, canonical, mobile, links and downloads as applicable. Observed harmful live defects require authorized containment; unavailable readback alone stays unverified. Keep versioned receipts and previous editions.

- When adopting the proposed UAQG2.0.0 breaking tier contract, pin the product profile separately from the specialist exam: paid/full, free/bounded diagnostic or bulk/screening. Lower cost narrows agreed coverage, never truth, privacy or review. Bind required checks and typed review receipts to every exact final artifact hash; preserve source locators, semantic page visuals, distinct link/access/media states and UNKNOWN time/cost. Paid/free material judgments need independent review. Validate every bulk record, review flags and a reproducible random sample, and keep unreviewed leads SCREENING. Preserve the six dimension meanings, weights and calibration gate; stop on missing access, exhausted allowance or a stale review. See the [tier contract and tests](https://github.com/dennisyu/local-service-spotlight-skills/blob/main/docs/audit-quality/tier-contracts.md).
- For every new audit governed by this pack, follow the [common execution gate](https://github.com/dennisyu/local-service-spotlight-skills/blob/main/docs/audit-quality/enforcement.md). Read current instructions in the actual runtime, retain all valid baseline detail, obtain exact-final independent review, and run `docs/audit-quality/verify_bundle.py` against the private final files and evidence snapshots before release and after delivery readback. Synthetic exercises never establish production acceptance. A bundle consistency result cannot certify source truth or supply missing calibration. Delivery plus verified filing is required; record runtime adoption and real-run results separately. Use the existing owner, publisher and review clock; no new access or job is implied.

No regular expression honestly certifies semantic fidelity, evidence quality or independent review. These are evidence-backed review gates; counters are screening aids. This public procedure is a proposed quality profile until its receiving owner records adoption; it grants no new account access, publication authority or schedules.

## Required real-media package for every human-facing audit

Dennis Yu, 18 November 2026: “Real pictures, videos, thumbnails, greatest hits,
and useful next steps should be required to earn an A.” This applies to every
agent and every human-facing audit: personal brand, SEO, GEO, security,
subscriptions, weekly MAA, ads, profiles and operations, regardless of price.

An A, A+ or A- requires genuine relevant source-bound photographs; verified real
video with its original accurate thumbnail/poster and exact usable watch link
(or a tested QR to that exact source in print); curated greatest hits stating
source, date, buyer relevance, intended reader and next use; and useful
source-linked next actions with owner/function, concrete first step, timeframe,
dependencies, authority, destination, done test and measurement when applicable.
Audio can add proof but cannot replace required video. Diagrams, screenshots,
charts and process maps add explanation; they cannot replace this package.
Stock/generated imagery cannot serve as observed proof. Counts cannot prove truth.

Independently inspect the final artifact, each included source and its relevance,
rights, identity, crop, accurate video/thumbnail pairing, greatest-hit selection
and action usefulness. Bind the review to exact final hash, physical page/section,
source evidence/capture and media IDs. Metadata discovery is not recording review;
record actual frames/transcript or muted recording inspection and never claim to
have heard/watched lessons from metadata alone. Test click/QR destinations and
intended-recipient access. A homepage, broken link or inaccessible private player
is insufficient. Historic media may be useful when clearly dated and relevant;
a stale capture/review or unrelated asset cannot earn current acceptance.

If any component cannot be verified, disclose the exact gap, required revision
and next verification; mark report quality A-INELIGIBLE / REVISION_REQUIRED.
There is no missing-media, N/A, “where useful”, or “only on request” A waiver.
Do not fabricate assets or lower the subject's reputation/SEO/security score
because the report lacks media. Keep specialist exams and numerical report
weights 25/25/20/15/10/5, threshold 85, calibration, privacy, authority and full
package gates intact. Pure machine/bulk outputs may stay SCREENING, with no A
or full-report claim. Historical receipts remain historical; legacy schemas
cannot accept new production reports. Synthetic tests are exercises, never real
runtime adoption or client proof. Source publication is not fleet activation.

The November 10 article publication fallback remains an article rule; it does
not waive this stricter audit A gate. Run the versioned typed contract and bundle
checks, then independent semantic review; a consistency PASS is not a truth certificate.

Verified video curation may rely on video identity, the original poster and an
explicitly attributed publisher description, with its limitation visible. This
cannot support a derived lesson, quote or observed behavior without inspecting
that recording location. Do not imply the full recording was watched. Typed
claim_basis distinguishes those cases. Final PDF source cards need both tested
digital clicks and a usable printed URL or decoded QR to the exact source.
