## Show and link examples when explaining a concept

- When explaining a concept (GCT, Content Factory, Dollar-a-Day, MAA, SAE, Nine Triangles,
  and the rest), always show at least one **concrete example** AND **link to a real
  page/task/article**.
- Prefer Task Library / LSS / blitzmetrics.com / dennisyu.com canonical URLs over invented
  examples. Example: Content Factory → https://blitzmetrics.com/content-factory/
- A definition with no example and no link is incomplete — add both before sending.
