## Quality and independent review

Rate the six dimensions using the pinned anchors; weights 25/25/20/15/10/5. UNKNOWN is null. Record each rating, evidence, exact contribution, assessed weight and total only when complete. Threshold 85; all-3 ratings total 75. Attach three calibration anchor decisions, reviewer disagreement and adjudication. An uncalibrated score remains provisional.

Record PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN/BLOCKED/N/A for each hard gate, with evidence. Record independent reviewer identity, fresh-context scope, exact candidate hash and separate content/privacy and design dispositions. Every retained S2 needs acceptance, risk reason, owner, deadline and completion test. Any material change reopens affected review.

## Delivery and verification

Record authorized route, exact preimage, source revision, rollback guard and save/deploy result. Private delivery requires recipient access and final-byte verification. Public web release additionally requires anonymous ordinary URL/body/canonical, source media, desktop/mobile, links and downloads. Do not publish private evidence to make a check convenient.

Separate RELEASE_READY, BLOCKED, DEPLOYED_UNVERIFIED, PUBLISHED_VERIFIED and PRIVATE_DELIVERED_VERIFIED. An observed harmful live defect triggers authorized containment and an incident receipt; an unavailable readback alone proves no harm. Record unresolved gaps, exact owner, next action and review date. Keep earlier editions.
