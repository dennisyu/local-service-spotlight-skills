Engagement tiers and capability routing

Proposed annex UAQG-TIERS 1.0.0 · 16 October 2026 · part of proposed UAQG 1.1.0. Status: candidate for independent review; not published, adopted or calibrated.

An audit should give its reader a supported decision and an action they can check. Agree the coverage and delivery format before choosing tools. The three products below use the same quality gates; a smaller allowance changes scope, not truth, identity, privacy or the meaning of acceptance.

Make the reasoning and action inspectable

Every material finding records evidence IDs and exact locations → interpretation and uncertainty → consequence for this goal and buyer → proposed action → test. Keep contrary evidence and UNKNOWN visible. A public self-claim establishes that the claim is published; it does not independently establish the claimed outcome.

Every action points back to a finding and evidence and names an accountable function, or an explicitly proposed role awaiting acceptance. Include first step, dependencies (including an explicit empty list), required authority, actual due date or proposed timeframe, destination, observable done-when test, and business metric with source/baseline or UNKNOWN. Proposed ownership, dates and benefits are not accepted commitments or measured results. No action executes solely because it appears in an audit. Reuse the existing task and MAA review instead of adding clocks.

Give each visual a job

For every human-facing PDF page record a semantic visual-function manifest: page, question answered, meaningful visual kind, source/evidence IDs, caption and exact render-review evidence. A proof map, annotated recording gallery, demand comparison, relationship diagram or conflict map should explain that page's finding. Decorative cards, bars, logo counts, link counts and ordinary text tables do not satisfy the existing every-human-page meaningful-visual requirement. Retain all-page renders, rights/provenance and readability checks. The validator can require manifests and review receipts; it cannot decide whether a diagram teaches something true.

For each link preserve four distinct states with method, evidence and time: reachable, expected_content, recipient_access, media_inspected. Use PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN/BLOCKED or justified N/A. HTTP200 is reachability only; a login page may have the wrong content or be inaccessible to the intended recipient. oEmbed metadata and thumbnails do not prove media inspection. A recording supporting a material claim needs inspected content at the cited location, or the claim stays limited/UNKNOWN and the affected required check holds. Browser playback must be muted with volume zero; use a bounded transcript or silent inspection when appropriate, and record what was actually inspected. Do not convert transcript review into video playback. Never claim all links usable because an availability check passed.

The four PASS assertions require different typed evidence, bound to the report hash and exact target URL: LINK_REACHABILITY may use HTTP or oEmbed metadata; LINK_CONTENT requires inspected HTML/document/transcript/video content; RECIPIENT_ACCESS requires an actual anonymous or authorized recipient read and a matching non-secret recipient_ref; MEDIA_CONTENT requires inspected media or its cited transcript. Metadata cannot stand in for content or recipient access. Delivery readback check names must be unique, and every asserted check must PASS; a later failure cannot be hidden by an earlier PASS. Unknown, duplicate or conflicting readback rows hold delivery verification.

Record actual executing app/device, model/effort if surfaced else UNKNOWN, code/tool revision, capability test evidence, access state and allowance for each routed stage. Bound attempts, waits and total cost. No universal model ranking, free-plan guarantee, autonomous uptime promise or paid service requirement is implied. If capability or budget is insufficient, hold the affected release and give the owner an actionable retrieval, repair or authorized rescope step.

Timing and acceptance are evidence, not badges

Separate brief/demo duration, draft generation, QA elapsed time, human minutes, agent runtime, waits, retries/rework and actual cost/currency. Each measured value needs its unit, method and evidence; missing values are null/UNKNOWN. Overlapping durations must not be added as if sequential. A reported stage demo duration does not measure end-to-end production, checking, export or delivery.

Paid/full and free/diagnostic acceptance composes this annex with existing UAQG hard gates and the original six report-quality dimensions, weights 25/25/20/15/10/5 and 85 threshold only with recorded calibration. Uncalibrated numbers remain provisional. Do not change scoring to accommodate cheaper work. The evidence-backed gate is evaluated per artifact, not a generic Reviewed badge. Each bulk record requires inspected MECHANICAL_VALIDATION evidence bound to that record ID and hash; an ordinary source row cannot establish validation. Open S2 defects need the same accepted disposition fields as other tiers; an incomplete screening run may retain its leads privately as INCOMPLETE/HOLD, but receives no successful SCREENING validation result. Bulk screening reports pipeline validation and coverage; it does not claim an accepted report-quality score or full-population independent review. Promoting a lead to a diagnostic/full product needs a new explicit profile and exact review.

Use the JSON contract, starter receipt, existing checker and examples. Mechanical checks validate asserted receipts, not source truth, reviewer independence or rendered quality. Keep receipts private when they identify clients, contacts, private sources or operational access.

Benchmark patterns, with their limitations

The supplied 20-page founder/business audit is a strong design exemplar, not a certified pass or a new exam. Useful patterns: pages1–2 decision-led proof, gap and action; page3 assumption-labeled goal-to-market reasoning; page6 ranked proof; page7 relationship map; page8 inspectable recording gallery; page12 comparable market demand; page15 entity/conflict diagram; page18 owned dated actions with approvals and done-when tests; page19 contradiction register.

Its page20 says the final v1.1 independent recheck was UNTESTED after a usage cap; the page19 Reviewed badge must not override that. Page5 pins historical Brand Authority v1.0 while the maintained method names v1.1. The supplied link receipt's 48/48 reachable does not establish recipient access (two need login) or inspected media (13 checks use oEmbed metadata). The reported520 links/102 bookmarks are not quality targets. Test intended destinations, inspectability and section navigation. Dennis's roughly three-minute on-stage account is reported demo timing; measured end-to-end production duration is UNKNOWN. Public teaching may use these bounded lessons; the supplied client benchmark and private receipts are not redistributed.
