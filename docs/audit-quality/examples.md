# Audit gate examples and calibration exercise

These examples are synthetic. They teach decision logic; they are not completed client audits, operational calibration or proof of business gains.

| Case | Evidence | Gate decision | Repair and retest |
|---|---|---|---|
| A polished but lossy document | Candidate opens, has more links, but omits two valid examples and a sourced comparison diagram | HOLD S1 fidelity; a high technical score cannot override loss | Restore examples/diagram around current verified facts; compare every baseline item, render all pages and review exact final hash |
| An outdated recommendation | New dated primary source explicitly retires an old tool recommendation | CORRECTED/OUTDATED, with source and change record | Preserve the dated history when useful; replace present-tense advice, propagate to summary and dependent downloads |
| Missing analytics access | Auditor cannot open owner analytics | UNKNOWN, not zero traffic and not a broken site | Deliver bounded public findings; record missing authorized analytics read and keep affected score incomplete |
| A 90-point report with a private contact in metadata | All dimensions look strong; hidden-content inspection finds unauthorized disclosure | S0 HOLD regardless of score | Remove from underlying metadata, rebuild and inspect final bytes; fresh privacy review. If already public, contain through authorized guarded action and record incident |
| Website published but readback times out | Deployment ID exists; no ordinary-URL check succeeds | DEPLOYED_UNVERIFIED | Reconcile served revision through the existing owner; do not claim live or assume damage |
| Wrong material claim observed live | Anonymous body shows the superseded total | INCIDENT_HOLD S1 | Stop promotion, notify owner, apply authorized guarded fix/rollback, verify corrected live body and every dependent output |
| Secondary chart label needs work | S2 remains with no accepted disposition | HOLD | Fix it or record explicit acceptance, risk, owner, deadline and retest. It cannot violate a hard gate |
| Private operational audit | Authorized recipient can open the exact checked file; hash matches | PRIVATE_DELIVERED_VERIFIED if all other applicable gates pass | No public URL or anonymous exposure is required |

## Three anchor artifacts to prepare before operational scoring

Choose three safe real artifacts with permission, or build reproducible synthetic artifacts: a clear fail with unsupported claims and missing figures; a borderline report with all facts and fidelity gates satisfied but ordinary presentation; a strong report with precise sources, preserved detail and useful comparison visuals. Supply the same source packet, profile and final bytes independently to each reviewer. Save each artifact/hash and each criterion-level rating, gate decision, disagreement and adjudication.

Arithmetic illustrations: all dimensions rated 3 yield 75. Ratings [4,3,3,4,3,3] yield exactly 85. Ratings [3,3,4,4,3,3] yield 83.75. Ratings [4,3,4,4,3,3] yield 90, but any S0/S1 or failed hard gate still holds. With citations UNKNOWN, this last vector has a known subtotal of 70 over 80 assessed weight and no total. These numbers demonstrate calculation only; they do not demonstrate that reviewers would assign those ratings reliably.
