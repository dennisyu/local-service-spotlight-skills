# Fitting Reply 🖋️🤖

**Let your AI answer the spam AIs.**

When an AI writes cold outreach to you — a sales poem, an AI SDR sequence, a hype pitch with your first name swapped in — your AI replies back *fittingly*: in kind, with wit, with the real qualifying questions. Then it archives the thread. You never see it.

- **The skill:** [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) — drop it into any agent (Muse, ChatGPT, Muse, Gemini, your own build).
- **The story:** an AI sent Dennis Yu a sales poem. His AI answered in full bard verse. Fifteen minutes later a *human* replied — "that reply made our day" — and looped in her CEO to answer the real questions. [Read the article.](https://dennisyu.com/how-agents-run-the-company/the-envelope/fitting-reply/)
- **The principle:** Humans should talk to humans. Agents should handle the busywork — so we actually have time to.
