# Exact 20-page visual PDF blueprint

The audit is exactly **20 rendered pages**. The evidence ledger, raw exports, transcripts, and
asset manifest are separate companion files, not appendix pages that change the count.
The page titles, numbers, and order below are the locked product contract; a missing, duplicate,
or reordered page fails acceptance.

## Visual contract for every page

- Use a consistent, accessible brand palette, type scale, grid, section marker, page number,
  and evidence footer.
- Every page contains at least one meaningful approved real photograph, source screenshot,
  data chart, or evidence-backed diagram. Logos, icons, decorative gradients, stock photos,
  and AI-generated portraits do not count as evidence.
- Caption visuals with what they establish, evidence IDs, source, and date. Do not infer an
  identity, location, relationship, or outcome from a filename or image alone.
- Prefer one clear takeaway, one primary visual, and a small number of supporting facts per
  page. Shrinking type to fit a wall of text fails.
- Use real-human imagery when available and permitted. Public availability is not permission
  to republish; otherwise use a sourced diagram or screenshot and flag the asset request.
- Keep `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, and `UNKNOWN` visually distinct without using color as the only
  signal. Put evidence IDs beside substantive claims.
- Do not fabricate chart values. Use `N/A` for non-applicable and `UNKNOWN` for unmeasured;
  never draw either as zero.

## Executive-summary rule

Pages 1–3 form the executive section, but page 1 stands alone. Do not open with a flattering
bio. Use the cover’s four facts and page 2’s deeper analysis for surprising evidence: an
authority asset stranded on another domain, a namesake taking the search result, an unsupported
claim, a relationship pattern, a buyer gap, or a company opportunity that changes the plan.

## Agent-install panel

Page 17 must show, at minimum, the audit’s routed skill stack:

`evidence-verification` → `positive-mentions-harvester` →
`reputation-gap-analyzer` → `geo-visibility-audit` →
`knowledge-panel-entity-seo` → `content-factory` →
`dollar-a-day-strategist`, with `model-judgment` governing execution tiers.

The QR is not proof of installation. Print the full HTTPS URL next to it, decode the rendered
QR, fetch the destination anonymously, and record the status and final URL in the acceptance
receipt.
