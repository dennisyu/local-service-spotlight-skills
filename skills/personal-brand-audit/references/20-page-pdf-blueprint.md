# Exact 20-page visual PDF blueprint

The audit is exactly **20 rendered pages**. The evidence ledger, raw exports, transcripts, and
asset manifest are separate companion files, not appendix pages that change the count.
Pages 1, 2, and 20 are invariant. Pages 3–19 may be reordered or combined when that improves the
subject-specific story, provided the run includes a coverage crosswalk from every default
requirement below to the actual rendered page or pages. Missing coverage, padding, or moving
substantive report content only into a companion file fails acceptance.

## Visual contract for every page

- Use a consistent, accessible brand palette, type scale, grid, section marker, page number,
  and evidence footer.
- Every page contains at least one meaningful approved real photograph, source screenshot,
  data chart, or evidence-backed diagram. Logos, icons, decorative gradients, stock photos,
  and AI-generated portraits do not count as evidence.
- Caption visuals with what they establish, evidence IDs, source, and date. Do not infer an
  identity, location, relationship, or outcome from a filename or image alone.
- Prefer one clear takeaway, one primary visual, and a small number of supporting facts per
  page. Shrinking type to fit a wall of text fails.
- Normal body type is 11–12pt; figure labels and evidence footnotes are at least 9pt.
  Inspect the exported pages at normal reading/print size. A full webpage reduced to an
  unreadable thumbnail cannot establish a finding: crop the relevant region, annotate it,
  and print its source/evidence ID. Readability formulas do not inspect the page.
- Declare an image-led visual brief before layout: visual roles, page-specific required
  imagery, composition families and how the normal-size review will judge it. Choose proof
  cards, source comparisons, networks, flows, charts and timelines for the jobs they serve.
  Reusing a grid is fine; a repetitive two-column memo cannot pass an image-led brief.
  Evaluate the actual pages against that brief. Do not add arbitrary photo quotas or invent
  data to fill a chart; visual diversity cannot excuse padding or incorrect evidence.
- Use real-human imagery when available and permitted. Public availability is not permission
  to republish; otherwise use a sourced diagram or screenshot and flag the asset request.
  That fallback supports a partial draft; page 1 still requires an approved real subject or
  documentary context photo. A website screenshot, logo, generated likeness or unrelated
  photo fails that invariant even if it passes geometry checks. A portrait is preferred when
  permissioned; a context photo must actually show relevant real people/work, with provenance.
- Keep `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, and `UNKNOWN` visually distinct without using color as the only
  signal. Put evidence IDs beside substantive claims.
- Do not fabricate chart values. Use `N/A` for non-applicable and `UNKNOWN` for unmeasured;
  never draw either as zero.

## Executive-summary rule

Pages 1–2 form the invariant executive section, and page 1 stands alone. A subject-specific
page 3 may extend the executive section when the coverage crosswalk says so. Do not open with a
flattering bio. Use the cover’s four facts and page 2’s deeper analysis for surprising evidence: an
authority asset stranded on another domain, a namesake taking the search result, an unsupported
claim, a relationship pattern, a buyer gap, or a company opportunity that changes the plan.

The first two or three sentences connect the subject's actual buyer or business situation,
the most useful supported finding, and the decision this audit enables. Explain the value before
the method. Keep the real photo and finding map beside that opening. Each subsequent page opens
with its specific takeaway. Generic praise, a repeated entrepreneur pitch, and unmeasured promises
of more conversions fail the shared specific-GCT opening standard.

## Agent-install panel

The installable-agent page or panel must show, at minimum, the audit’s routed skill stack and
must be mapped in the coverage crosswalk:

`evidence-verification` → `positive-mentions-harvester` →
`reputation-gap-analyzer` → `geo-visibility-audit` →
`knowledge-panel-entity-seo` → `content-factory` →
`dollar-a-day-strategist`, with `model-judgment` governing execution tiers.

The QR is not proof of installation. Print the full HTTPS URL next to it, decode the rendered
QR, fetch the destination anonymously, and record the status and final URL in the acceptance
receipt.

When promised, the personalized kit is a separate delivery target, not a replacement install
rail. Include START-HERE, subject facts/UNKNOWNs, versioned contents manifest, source/evidence
map, runnable skill instructions, first real task, worked output example, permission gates
and receipt forms. Populate requests with this subject's actual evidence IDs, input locators,
expected outputs and gates; show ordered handoffs and the weekly operating rhythm. Explain
every promised skill. Do not call generic recipe links a personalized kit. No package
guarantee, automatic installation or running agent is implied.

### Worked handoff shape (synthetic teaching example)

Suppose an authorized brief for **Avery Sample, a fictional studio owner**, asks for more
qualified consultations in 90 days. E-001 is a permissioned interview; E-002 is a dated
customer quote; the current inquiry baseline is UNKNOWN. These rows illustrate usable
requests, not real customer facts or promised business results:

| Skill / order | Populated starter request | Output, gate and receipt |
|---|---|---|
| `evidence-verification` / first | Check E-001 and E-002 against their retained extracts; separate the customer's words from Avery's claims; list any missing consent or outcome record. | A dated proof card with evidence IDs and UNKNOWNs; research hands accepted proof to content; retain source hashes and reviewer verdict. |
| `content-factory` / after proof review | Use approved E-001 interview topics and E-002 quote to draft one consultation-page article and a short-clip brief pointing to the existing consultation URL. No invented quote or edit timecode. | Article/clip draft plus Produce → Process → Post → Promote handoff; publication/reuse gates remain; content records exact draft and source revisions. |
| `weekly-brand-maa` / after measurement access | Compare the approved consultation goal with the authorized inquiry export for the stated week; keep baseline UNKNOWN until the export establishes it; name the one next test. | Metrics → Analysis → Action with definitions, source period and limitations; analytics hands the result to the implementation owner; no schedule or lead-growth claim without receipts. |

The kit's first task is the proof card, with its worked output beside the request. The weekly
cadence is a proposed operating rhythm until its owner and scheduler are confirmed. A fresh
agent should be able to produce that first private output from the supplied files without
asking Dennis to reconstruct the brief. Existing canonical skills and bespoke wrappers must
be mapped explicitly; a wrapper name does not certify a marketplace installation.

Decode both QRs from the final PDF. For the kit, test the intended recipient's access to the
landing page and download, then open the actual downloaded package and match its contents/hash
to the staged version. A 200 login page, generic install page, owner-only share or wrong client's
kit fails. Record test identity class (anonymous or authorized recipient), time and actual
result without storing credentials. If recipient access or hosting is unresolved, leave HOLD
and supply the local companion for review; do not mint a delivery pass.
