# Intake and authority boundary

Ask all blocking questions before the long research run. Continue outside-in when private or
credentialed inputs are unavailable, but make the missing lanes visible.

## Minimum intake

| Topic | Required answer or explicit UNKNOWN |
|---|---|
| Subject | Preferred name, legal/professional variants, pronouns, headshot, current role and company |
| Identity anchors | Two identifiers that distinguish the subject from namesakes |
| Buy box | Who should hire, invite, buy from, partner with, or introduce the subject; for what outcome |
| Offers and outcomes | Current offers, price/fit boundaries, desired business result, capacity |
| Geography and language | Markets, languages, transliterations, former locations that may appear in records |
| Owned surfaces | Personal/company domains, social profiles, channels, newsletter, podcast, books, courses |
| Proof seeds | Clients, outcomes, publications, talks, awards, associations, interviews, reviews |
| Relationship seeds | People the subject believes are close or warm, plus what may be said publicly |
| Source assets | Photos, recordings, transcripts, decks, analytics, testimonials, permissions |
| Comparisons | Two to five peers or competitors and why they win the desired opportunity |
| Constraints | Private topics, embargoes, confidential clients, regulated claims, prohibited likenesses |
| Delivery | Recipient, intended channel, deadline, file format, and who accepts the result |
| Action authority | Public research, private-source reading, credentialed reading, drafting, publishing, sending, spending, and installation — each separately |

Do not make the audit wait for optional private access. Produce an outside-in result and a
precise request list for the credentialed delta.

## Four authority lanes

### 1. Public outside-in — default

Allowed without login: search, fetch public pages, inspect metadata/schema/crawl files, use
public transcripts and captions, read public reviews, and draft analysis. Data that happens to
be publicly exposed can still be private or unsafe; minimize it and route security-sensitive
findings privately rather than reproducing them in the brand report.

### 2. User-supplied or private sources — scoped reading

Use only files, messages, exports, and relationship facts the requester placed in scope.
Label them private. A private text can establish that two people communicated; it does not
make the conversation publishable or authorize contact. Obtain explicit reuse permission for
quotes, screenshots, photos, attendee records, customer details, and personal contact data.

### 3. Credentialed inside-out — explicit access

Analytics, Search Console, social insights, CRM, CMS, ad platforms, email, file stores, and
profile settings require authorized access to the exact account and requested read scope.
Verify the account/property before reading. Do not bypass authentication, guess credentials,
or infer that one account grants access to another.

The credentialed lane reports first-party performance; it does not overwrite what anonymous
buyers and search/AI systems see outside-in.

### 4. External action — separate approval

Research and document creation do not authorize:

- sending email, DMs, texts, invites, or follow-ups;
- publishing or editing pages, profiles, schema, posts, videos, or reviews;
- spending, launching, pausing, killing, or scaling advertising;
- installing, enabling, scheduling, or granting an agent access;
- changing credentials, DNS, billing, legal claims, or permissions;
- describing a private connection or using a person’s likeness publicly.

Stage the exact action, destination, account, audience, cost, dates, and read-back test for a
human decision.

## Data minimization

- Do not copy personal email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, attendee lists, access
  tokens, or private-message content into a public audit unless disclosure is necessary,
  lawful, and explicitly approved.
- Use a source locator rather than embedding private data in the ledger.
- Treat exposed private data as a security finding, not a marketing asset.
- Separate allegations from adjudicated findings and include the organization’s response when
  materially relevant.
- Public availability does not grant image, quote, trademark, or testimonial reuse rights.

## State vocabulary

Record the most advanced state actually evidenced. States in different lanes are not
substitutes.

### Work-product lane

- **RESEARCHED:** source collection and ledger exist.
- **DRAFTED:** editable narrative/plan exists.
- **GENERATED:** document source and assets exist.
- **RENDERED:** the intended PDF/file was produced and opened.
- **REVIEWED:** named reviewer checked the exact rendered artifact against the acceptance suite.

### Delivery/publication lane

- **STAGED:** final payload and exact destination are ready for approval.
- **APPROVED:** a named human approved that payload and destination.
- **SENT / PUBLISHED:** source system records the action.
- **DELIVERED:** destination accepted the transport; not the same as recipient review.
- **RECEIVED:** the intended recipient acknowledges the exact artifact, or a recipient-side
  read/view receipt proves it was opened. A sent-folder copy, sender-side read-back, HTTP 200,
  or transport acceptance proves at most SENT, PUBLISHED, or DELIVERED—not RECEIVED.

### Agent lifecycle lane

- **AVAILABLE:** the skill exists in a merged marketplace commit.
- **INSTALLED:** the named account shows the bundle/skill at that commit or version.
- **ENABLED:** the named account allows the skill to run.
- **TESTED:** a fresh chat activated it and passed a realistic request.
- **SCHEDULED:** a job definition exists with timezone and owner.
- **OBSERVED:** a scheduled firing produced a timestamped success or failure receipt.

Never report a local file as delivered, a QR code as installation, a merged skill as installed,
or a schedule definition as an observed run.

## Stop and escalate

Stop the affected lane, preserve the rest of the audit, and name the smallest blocker when:

- identity cannot be resolved to two independent attributes;
- sources expose credentials or private personal data;
- a legal, medical, financial, employment, or regulated claim lacks suitable evidence;
- the subject disputes a material fact and no source of truth is designated;
- relationship depth or likeness permission would change the public output;
- the only path requires login bypass, unapproved contact, publication, spend, or access change.
