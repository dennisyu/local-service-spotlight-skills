# Brand Authority rubric v1.0

Version: **1.0 — 4 September 2026**

Use this rubric only for the ten-row Brand Authority exam. Cite evidence IDs beneath every
row. Do not change the categories, weights, or 80/100 pass line inside a client report. A
future rubric change requires a new version and scores from different versions must not be
shown as a direct trend.

## Shared scoring anchors

Apply the row-specific evidence test below, then choose the closest anchor:

| Score | Evidence standard |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Adequate, recorded coverage establishes an active false, broken, or misidentifying condition that materially harms correct identity or buyer action. Mere absence is 1, never 0. |
| 1 | Adequate, recorded coverage establishes that the capability is absent, without an active harmful contradiction. Never use 1 for an unchecked or inaccessible source. |
| 2 | Only a fragment or self-claim exists; it is stale, isolated, or lacks independent/public proof, but it is more than complete absence. |
| 3–4 | A basic asset or isolated corroboration exists, but important identity, recency, consistency, or buyer-utility gaps remain. |
| 5–6 | A credible foundation is observable and partly corroborated, but coverage, distribution, conversion, or entity linkage is incomplete. |
| 7–8 | Strong, current, independently corroborated evidence is easy to find; only bounded gaps prevent category-leading proof. |
| 9–10 | The row's high anchor is fully evidenced across the relevant surfaces, current, internally consistent, and connected to the entity home and buy box. |

Use an odd score when the evidence fits the lower anchor band and an even score when it fits
the upper end of that band. Document the one-sentence reason. If coverage is insufficient to
distinguish 0 from missing evidence, score **UNKNOWN**, not 0.

## Row-specific low and high anchors

| # | Category | Score 1: verified absence | Score 2: weak fragment | Score 9–10: high anchor |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entity home and name rank | No owned home after qualified-name/domain coverage | Placeholder or thin owned page exists but does not resolve the name SERP | A facts-first owned name domain is live, unambiguous, and ranks first for the qualified name |
| 2 | Media relationships and offline authority | No dated third-party appearance, stage, press, or institution proof | One stale, low-relevance, or self-asserted appearance lead | Multiple current, named stages/media relationships are independently verifiable and relevant to the buy box |
| 3 | Content assets | No substantive attributable content asset found | Thin/stale inventory or substantive assets only on an employer/host domain | A current, owned library includes definitive pages plus dated shows, talks, book/course, or equivalent flagship assets |
| 4 | Social proof | No attributable testimonial, outcome, review, or audience proof found | An anonymous, stale, isolated, or self-published proof fragment | Named testimonials/outcomes and openable current audience proof corroborate the desired buyer belief |
| 5 | Monetizable positioning | No visible way to hire, buy, book, refer, or inquire | Generic contact route exists without clear audience, problem, offer, or proof | The entity home makes the qualified audience, problem, offer, proof, and next step immediately clear |
| 6 | Consistency and schema hygiene | No owned identity record or Person/entity markup found | Partial, stale, or inconsistent bios/schema exist without a stable identity node | One stable Person identity and `@id` connect consistent bios, titles, schema, navigation, and canonical profiles |
| 7 | Current-channel momentum | No current publishing or distribution found in the defined window | Sporadic recent activity exists without reliable reach or engagement evidence | Recent source-backed publishing reaches and engages the intended audience consistently across priority channels |
| 8 | Link authority to entity home | No credible referring domain points to the entity home after junk exclusion | One or a few weak/relevant links exist, or most authority points elsewhere | Diverse authoritative domains link to the correct entity home in relevant editorial context |
| 9 | Search visibility beyond name | No non-name discovery found in the defined query/data coverage | One weak or low-ranking non-name discovery signal exists | Multiple non-name topics/pages rank or drive evidenced qualified discovery tied to the buy box |
| 10 | Knowledge Graph presence | No reliable machine-readable identity found after graph/entity coverage | A weak, uncorroborated, incomplete, or split identity candidate exists | The correct person has one corroborated machine-readable identity, stable KGMID/graph presence, and an eligible or claimable panel |

Use 0 instead of 1 only when the evidence shows active harm—for example, an owned identity
home points to the wrong person, a broken primary conversion route blocks action, or the live
graph merges the subject with a namesake. Name the harmful condition and evidence IDs.

## Scoring receipt

For each row record: rubric version, score or UNKNOWN, coverage performed, evidence IDs,
anchor selected, interpolation reason, capture date, and reviewer. Add the numeric rows only.
If any required row is UNKNOWN, show the known subtotal and **INCOMPLETE — no pass verdict**.
