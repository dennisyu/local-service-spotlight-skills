# Audit run contract v1.0

Version: **AUDIT-METHOD-2026.10.02**. Public teaching edition:
[How We Audit](https://blitzmetrics.com/how-we-audit/).
This is the shared run procedure. Specialist skills own their exam rules; this
contract does not create a composite score or supersede the implementation master.

## 1. Freeze the job before researching

Record a stable subject ID, verified identity, company/market/language, business
goal and time window, offer, ideal customer, price when supplied, and desired
decision. Begin with authorized calls, leads, CRM, bookings and revenue records
when available. If they are unavailable, continue the public proof audit and mark
the business measurements UNKNOWN. Followers and search visibility do not prove sales.

Pin one input packet and output contract: exam family, rubric ID/version, exact
source revision/hash, rows/weights, arithmetic, UNKNOWN and N/A rules, pass line,
report template/version, requested length, audience, source permissions,
subscription/usage allowance, retries and total stop rule. A different exam is
not a new version of the same exam. Changing an exam requires a new explicit choice.
Do not infer a Reputation-to-Sales Gap rubric from Brand Authority, SEO/Growth,
the 30-point proof-item scale, a planning document, or a historic report.

The maintained personal-brand-audit product uses its 20-page blueprint. Historical
15-page and adaptive 15/20-page instructions remain historical output contracts.
Length is selected for the actual engagement; a comparison's agreed exact length
is a comparison control, not proof of a universal historic rule. Record any
authorized template exception before work and retain the coverage crosswalk.

### Base audit first; relevant GCT clarification is optional

For premium/conference work, deliver a base audit from existing evidence before
asking for strategy answers. Resolve identity before attributing proof. Goals,
Content, Targeting (GCT) means the result sought, proof available and buyers served;
missing GCT is not missing reputation and must not lower its score.

Store each GCT value, source, supplied/inferred/UNKNOWN state, confidence and the
recommendation it changes. If confirmed information is clear, proceed without a
repeat questionnaire. If evidence suggests a direction, label it INFERRED and
offer Confirm / Edit / Continue with base audit. Ask at most three relevant
questions in the first pass, only when different answers change the chosen
proof, priority, channel, offer or measurement. Explain that effect in plain words.

Useful questions, shown only when needed: What would make the next 90 days a win?
Whose trust do you need, and what should they buy or do? Which one to three
results, reviews, talks or recordings should those people see? Public links or
short voice/text answers suffice; do not require new analytics access or logins.

Keep Continue with base audit visible. Skipping or “not sure” produces qualified
findings and conditional priorities, with the open sales destination carried to
the workshop. It does not block all value or permit a final sales diagnosis or
revenue forecast from an unconfirmed goal. Preserve the base version. On reply,
revise only affected findings, proof ranks and actions; show what changed and why.
Deliver the useful preparation snapshot before the conference when authorized.
One permissioned attendee register tracks identity, base revision, GCT status,
questions/reasons, reply/skip, revised report, decisions and delivery/QA receipts.
Attendance and audit readiness are separate. Ownership acceptance and organizer
coordination precede intake or bulk delivery; a method update does not start them.

## 2. Build one proof inventory

Use one editable ledger, linked from the report. Each row has a stable evidence ID,
subject match, source URL or restricted locator, publisher/source class, date of
the event, capture time, what the source establishes, permission, and freshness.
Keep OBSERVED, INFERRED and UNKNOWN explicit. A quoted sales claim is observed
as a claim, not automatically independently verified. Preserve contradictory
sources until the owning record resolves them. Do not invent a missing asset.

Inventory real recordings, appearances, articles, reviews, outcomes, talks,
relationships and existing offers. Deduplicate the same source/moment. Rank
eligible proof with the maintained Who / Where / What 30-point scale and explain
its relevance to the buyer. This ranks individual proof, not the whole business.
Record public association separately from friendship, endorsement and introduction
permission. Reuse the evidence-ledger identity and privacy controls.

### Source census and derivative inventory

Declare each source, surface, date, access boundary, pagination completion and
counted unit. A complete current public channel listing is not a lifetime census.
Feed releases, unique conversations, host/guest roles, full filmed interviews,
audio releases, clips, Shorts, event companions and website posts stay distinct.
Use stable publisher IDs. Group alternate editions only with evidence; never
deduplicate by episode number alone. Preserve cross-role republications and
multipart/compilation relationships. A video not found is not proven audio-only;
a publisher upload does not establish full moving footage. Blocked checks are
unresolved, not deleted. Keep unresolved leads outside confirmed totals.

Link source releases to derivatives with explicit relationship and match evidence.
Published sources are not private raw camera/audio masters. Record raw-file
holdings UNKNOWN unless that repository was actually inventoried. Do not divide
all uploads by feed releases and call it a repurposing ratio. Separate exact
watch-page views, rounded listing views, capture dates and missing date coverage;
do not invent velocity. Rankings explain the measured population and buyer fit;
views, likes and audience size do not prove inquiries, revenue or cause.

Reconcile the current product, business goal, readiness and marketing authority
from first-party records. Historical project names, a stale launch promise or a
public discussion do not prove the current offer/ownership. Keep equipment,
podcast partnership and a separate product venture in distinct lanes until their
identity and objectives are confirmed. Existing hubs, sponsor offers and reported
automations require source/output/CTA/measurement verification before recommending
their creation anew. Preserve the existing inventory instead of overwriting it.

## 3. Diagnose the gap against the goal

Compare what buyers need to believe with the proof that exists and what they can
actually find. Distinguish missing proof, buried proof, inconsistent identity,
weak positioning, broken conversion, missing measurement and inaccessible data.
Identify strengths worth using, meaningful counterevidence, namesakes and the
highest-value assets. Every material finding cites evidence; every inference
explains its support and limit.

Use the existing Money Tree to connect content leaves to buying/service branches
and the entity home. Link actual destinations, expose holes, and mark suggested
paths as proposed. Unconnected outcomes remain UNKNOWN; a link is not a sale.
Keep private revenue, leads and client priorities out of the public teaching edition.

For entity research keep graph-object identity/KGMID, normal-query visible panel
and owner claim status as three separate fields and receipts. A graph match score
is query relevance, not business authority. A forced KGMID display is not ordinary
name-query visibility. Blocked Google access remains UNKNOWN, never “no panel.”
Use the specialist's query, locale, device, time and owner-evidence requirements.

## 4. Score only the selected exam

Keep Brand Authority ten-row, SEO/Growth seven-component, Website QA checklist,
local/GBP products and Reputation-to-Sales Gap separate. Score using the exact
pinned rubric's anchors. Check the arithmetic in code. A headline must equal the
row sum or documented weighted calculation; preserve any explicit cap separately
from the raw total. UNKNOWN is null, never zero, absence or failure. If a required
row is UNKNOWN, show a known subtotal/coverage and INCOMPLETE with no pass verdict;
do not renormalize the remaining weights. N/A changes the denominator only when
the pinned rubric explicitly permits it.

Re-audit deltas require the same family, rubric version and comparable coverage.
Separate new evidence, an error correction and a changed rubric. None by itself
proves a business gain. Retain historic wrong totals as historic records with a
correction receipt; never use them as the next baseline.

## 5. Make the report implementable

The first screen answers the subject's business question. Show the strongest
supported finding and useful visual, strengths/gaps, limits and next decisions.
Use real permitted media, evidence-backed diagrams, a money tree, relationships,
and readable timelines where useful. Keep citations beside the claims. Do not
pad pages or substitute a long inventory for analysis.

### Composed report and actual visual acceptance

Use the supplied reference reports as design evidence, not transferable facts,
scores or proof of their author's model. The first two pages stand alone: the
business question, current strongest findings, a useful source-based visual and
the first decisions. Each major section has a finding headline, implication,
visual where useful, analysis/action and adjacent evidence IDs. Each major action
traces observation → consequence → next step → owner/function → measure. Use
varied evidence forms when supported: directly labelled bars, source screens,
relationship/source maps, proof cards, destination flows and a gated roadmap.
Do not invent a metric merely to fill a chart. Targets, company claims, inference
and UNKNOWN remain explicit. Keep current evidence separate from historic values.

Compose physical pages deliberately. Normal body type is 11–12pt; figure labels
and footnotes at least9pt. Use readable contrast and at most two embedded fonts.
Avoid tiny multi-column tables, accidental continuations, near-blank score pages
and page-count padding. Adapt length to substance and the agreed engagement,
retaining any comparison's exact template control. Printed numbers, contents and
physical PDF pages must agree. Open every final exported page, confirm no clipped
or overlapping text, selectable text, accurate labels/scales and usable links,
and bind that review to the final PDF hash. Geometry checks alone do not certify
visual or source truth. Public teaching uses a separately checked privacy edition
and never gains access to private source rows by sharing the operational file.

Prioritize actions by plausible business impact, effort and dependencies; label
estimates as assumptions. Every action has an ID, finding/evidence IDs, durable
owning function and responsible operator, routed skill, inputs/dependencies,
destination, due date, required authority, and an observable acceptance test.
Separate agent-executable work from decisions or access only a human can provide.
An audit recommendation does not authorize publication, spending or an access change.

Route specialist work through the maintained personal-brand-audit sequence. Use
Content Factory's Produce → Process → Post → Promote; stage Dollar-a-Day only for
proven organic winners within authorized account/budget limits. Plan implementation
and a source-backed weekly Metrics → Analysis → Action review using weekly-brand-maa
and measurement-analytics. Use the existing owner/task/clock, not a duplicate schedule.
Record the first verified change and business outcome separately from the report.

## 6. Reconcile and verify before releasing a batch

One coordinator owns the frozen packet, shared-fact ledger and final integration.
Workers return evidence IDs, uncertainties and exact artifact revisions. Log actual
visible skill reads, tool use and handoffs separately from requested skills or
assistant claims. Record platform, UI/provider-reported model and effort or UNKNOWN,
start/end time, allowance constraints, failures, retries and human rework. A historical
routing file is not proof of the historical executing model or hidden reasoning.

Correct the authoritative evidence/shared fact first. Give it a new revision, list
every dependent summary, leaderboard, HTML and PDF, regenerate them, and inspect
their final text and rendered pages. Reject any final artifact using a retired
shared claim, missing dependency, stale revision or old headline. Page counts and
worker self-QA do not prove visual or factual acceptance. The coordinator's review
and a fresh-context review have separate receipts and exact artifact hashes.

Use `scripts/audit_report_contract.py` with an approved rubric manifest and report
JSON to check arithmetic, UNKNOWN, version and entity/panel receipts. Its batch mode
checks the complete expected artifact list against the current shared facts and
retired text. PDF entries bind their binary hash to freshly extracted text using
`pypdf` (record its installed version); unavailable extraction keeps the batch HOLD. It is a deterministic
gate, not a source-truth or visual certificate.
Use `scripts/audit_inventory_contract.py` for the separate census and PDF receipt.
Its synthetic fixture is `tests/fixtures/audit-inventory-and-render/valid-synthetic.json`.
It rejects cross-unit or overlapping sums, UNKNOWN converted to a number,
unsupported parentage, a reuse yield with unknown raw holdings, missing physical
page reviews, small type and accepted-artifact hash drift. It validates declarations
and receipt completeness; it cannot establish that the sources or visual judgment
are true. The actual source enumeration and page inspection remain required.
Never run the comparison before the identities, common inputs/rubric/template and
ownership are agreed. Subscription-only comparisons use existing supported access;
missing tools are findings, not permission to buy APIs.
Identical instructions compare the actual workflows; they do not isolate model
effects from context, access and tools. A same-model paired instruction experiment
is a separate proposal requiring agreed runs, not an extra authorized pilot.

Release only within the current scope and through the freshly verified site route.
Retain the preimage, exact source/output revisions, rollback and public readback.
Open every rendered page/viewport and verify links at the actual destination.
Public methods omit private transcripts, customer details and operational access.
An updated GitHub source, installed skill, verified report, public page and weekly
business outcome each require their own receipt.
