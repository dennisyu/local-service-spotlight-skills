# Personal brand voice

**Use this when** you are writing or rewriting copy on a personal-brand site — the owner's articles, `/people/{slug}/` honor cards, or a guest post — and you need the voice and photo rule before you draft.

This is the voice rule. It is not a second website-builder and not a second people-directory. Point those skills here instead of restating the rule.

## The rule

- **Articles by the person on a personal-brand site:** first person, intimate.
- **People / honor cards (`/people/{slug}/`):** third person, still with real stories and real photos.
- **Guest posts:** first person as the guest, labeled guest.
- **Never invent photos or generate images of real people.**

If you do not have a real photograph of the people involved, publish without an image and say the gallery is coming. Do not make one.

## Inputs

- Which surface you are writing: owner article, honor card, or guest post.
- Real facts, dates, names, and (if any) real photographs already in hand.
- The existing page, if one exists — improve it; do not publish a competitor.

## Steps

1. **Name the surface.** Owner article, `/people/{slug}/` card, or guest post. If you cannot name it, stop and ask.
2. **Set the person.** First person for the owner's own articles. Third person for honor cards. First person as the guest, with a visible guest label, for guest posts.
3. **Keep the stories real.** Intimate is not invented. Use named events, dates, and photographs that already exist.
4. **Photos.** Use a real photo of the real people, or no photo. Never generate a likeness. Never use stock as a stand-in for a named person.
5. **Do not fight an in-flight rewrite.** If the live page is already being rewritten, improve that page or wait. Do not open a second URL on the same person.

## Definition of done

- Grammatical person matches the surface above.
- Guest posts are labeled guest.
- Every photograph on the page is a real image of the real people, or the page honestly has no photo.
- No generated likenesses, no invented “memories,” no second hub or second card.
- Sibling skills (`definitive-article-writer`, and the website / people-directory skills) point here rather than carrying their own copy of this rule.

## Worked example

https://dennisyu.com/people/chris-rummel/ — a `/people/{slug}/` honor card, written in the third person, with a real photograph from the library. That page is being rewritten; do not fight the rewrite and do not publish a competing Chris page.

How the cards are built: https://dennisyu.com/how-we-build-people-pages/

## Failure modes

- Writing an honor card in first person (“I met Chris…”) as if the owner were the subject of the card.
- Writing the owner's own essay in third person (“Dennis believes…”) on the owner's site.
- Publishing a guest post in the host's voice with no guest label.
- Generating an image of a real person because no photo was on hand.
- Opening a second article on the same person because the live one is mid-rewrite.

## Output

- Copy in the correct person, on the existing URL, with only real photographs.
- A one-line note in the receipt: surface, person used, photo yes/no, and that this skill was followed.
