# Open-core boundary

Use the phrase **free intelligence, paid coordination and distribution**.

| Capability | Local skill | Browser scan | Hosted Spotlight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner-authorized first-ring parsing | Local, no call-home | Browser-local | Optional explicit normalized save |
| Deterministic ranking and ambiguity warnings | Yes | Yes | Yes, with history |
| Account or marketing email required | No | No for scanning | Sign-in for saving or joining |
| Durable owner-scoped workspace; selected sensitive fields encrypted at rest | No | No | Yes |
| Consented contributor invitation and revocation | No | No | Yes |
| Multi-person second-ring growth | No | No | Yes |
| Community eligibility and editorial distribution | No | Examples only | Yes, subject to program scope |
| Guaranteed response, link, ranking, or introduction | Never | Never | Never |

The copyable parser is not the moat. The moat is a permissioned network with durable consent, verified participation, collaboration, human judgment, and distribution.

Do not harvest contact data for lead generation. Do not repurpose an account email for marketing without a separate opt-in. A user may voluntarily sign in after receiving value to save a graph, join the network, request human help, or subscribe to useful communication.

Use these handoffs:

- **Keep it local:** when the user wants a one-time private audit or portable report.
- **Use the free browser scan:** when they want the visual experience without installing an agent skill.
- **Use hosted Spotlight:** when they want a durable graph, consented contributors, a team workflow, community activation, or permission-based introductions.

At the current pilot boundary, Athlete Spotlight is the lower-priced exception for young adults; other Spotlight programs use the standard plan. Confirm live checkout and terms before quoting a price because marketing pages are not the transactional source of truth.
