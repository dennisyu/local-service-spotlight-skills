# GBP report format

Owner-facing. Plain words, real numbers, comparison on every number, under 550 words of prose, at most one em-dash. Charts derive from the report's own numbers only.

## Sections

**1. Maps pulse** (3–4 sentences). The primary action this period vs last with % change, in the owner's word. Maps visibility (maps impressions) vs last. The share of people who acted (action rate). One sentence on momentum from the 13-week trend ("held between 2 and 5 calls a week all summer").

**2. The numbers, shown.** A 13-week trend table or chart of the primary action. Then a small table: Calls · Website visits · Directions · (Messages, Bookings if non-zero) · Maps views · Search views, each with this period, last period, change.

**3. What people searched.** Top 5 search terms with impressions; one line on brand vs non-brand ("about 6 in 10 people found you by searching your name; the rest searched for [top non-brand term]"). Terms Google reports as "fewer than N" say so.

**4. Reviews.** New reviews this period vs last, average of new reviews vs overall, reply coverage, and any unanswered negative named by date and stars (never quote the reviewer).

**5. What it means.** The decomposed driver of any material swing (Phase 2.6). Hygiene opportunities as plain sentences with hedged effect: "Your profile lists no services. Adding the ten you quote most should help Google show you for those searches."

**6. What to do next.** 2–3 items. ✅ our-side forward commitments vs client-side asks (each with the specific thing: "reply to the 2-star review from Sept 3").

**7. Start here.** One move.

## Trial report (Phase 8)

Header (business, trial dates, duration, baseline description). Metrics table: Metric · 3-period average · Trial total · % lift, with Total Maps Impressions and Total Actions rows in bold. Map scans placeholder block for the human to fill. Analysis: bottom line on the primary, visibility on maps impressions, synthesis. Action: retention recommendation on CLEAR WIN or VISIBILITY WIN (mandatory opener: "Based on these results, we strongly recommend continued enrollment in the Maps Visibility System."), the fixed not-a-fit text on FAILURE, and an honest "inconclusive, here is what would settle it" on the stable band. Ends after the Action section.

## Recurring-run voice

Report fix progress on known issues ("the category fix from two weeks ago is live; searches for 'painter near me' rose from 41 to 66"). Raise only new asks. Never invent a ✅ for a week we did not act.
