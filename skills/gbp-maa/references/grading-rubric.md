# GBP report grading rubric

Grade A requires all nine; one revision on any fail; deliver with the failure flagged if it still fails.

1. **Primary first.** Opens with the locked primary action, comparison, % change, in the owner's word.
2. **Every number compared.** No naked numbers. Sums match headlines (actions by type = total actions; four surfaces = total impressions).
3. **Swing decomposed.** Any ≥ 20% move on the primary names the largest mover from the working table.
4. **Grounded prescriptions.** Every fix maps to an H-flag with pulled evidence or to a stated hedge. No invented Google features.
5. **Reviews handled.** Velocity, rating, reply coverage present; unanswered negatives named by date; no reviewer quotes.
6. **Search terms present.** Top 5 with numbers, brand vs non-brand stated, below-threshold terms reported as "fewer than N".
7. **Machinery contained.** Zero hits on banned vocabulary; no tool, proxy, or pipeline names.
8. **Actions are forward work.** ✅ only on commitments; asks are specific.
9. **Voice.** Under 550 prose words, ≤ 1 em-dash, no AI tells, reads as written by a person who looked at the profile.

Auto-penalties: a number not in the working table (F); a location reported by name match instead of location_id (F); grading a trial before the lag cleared (F); any claim that GBP proves booked jobs or revenue (D).
