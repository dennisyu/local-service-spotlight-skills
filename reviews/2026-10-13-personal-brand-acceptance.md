## Supplied-goal and proof-inventory follow-up

The earlier opening rule connected situation, value and decision, but did not explicitly
require a supplied client goal to lead page 1. The source amendment now binds the authorized
goal capture to the declared complete first 2–3 sentences and a fresh-context semantic
fidelity receipt. A goal only on page 3, a generic reputation frame, invented source wording
or a lexical-fragment receipt cannot satisfy that check. Safe paraphrasing remains possible;
the private supplied wording is not copied into public fixtures. Inferred and UNKNOWN goals
remain labelled and do not lower evidence or authority ratings.

Ranked proof rows now carry category, supporting evidence and PDF claim IDs, what the source
establishes, goal relevance, limitations, permission, next action IDs and the maintained
Who / Where / What components, rationales and sum. Linked claims/actions must carry the item's
supporting evidence. These are asset-level analyst ratings, separate from aggregate Brand
Authority and measured business outcomes. Incomplete components/totals remain null; unknown
goal fit carries a reason, owner and next evidence. No new weights or goal-fit composite exists.

Coauthor photos are engagement-specific. Their source/identity/permission checks apply when
present; they cannot replace the required subject/context cover photo and are not required
for other engagements. The supplied-goal lesson amends the existing GCT opening standard and
its distributed copies rather than creating a new SOP.
