The initial 33-test implementation exposed real gaps. The reviewer reproduced stale source,
photo, permission and brief reviews; a waived kit promise; generic kit role reuse; missing
per-skill locations; declaration-only install verification; rejected legitimate private
locators; and an unhandled malformed PDF. Follow-up review found a missing delivered manifest
and UNKNOWN author context. All were corrected and covered by regression cases.

The final checker binds the entire input manifest to review, checks brief-based applicability,
captures and distinct count units, approved cover photo declarations, every physical page
review, actual kit members/hashes, instruction and output mappings, delivered manifest version,
rendered-QR decoder receipts, recipient/download receipts and captured installation guide/
marketplace evidence. Fixtures are entirely synthetic. Mechanical PASS always returns
REVIEW_REQUIRED. Authentication of receipts, source truth, rights, visual composition,
complete claim enumeration, substantive strategy and live access still need actual review.
